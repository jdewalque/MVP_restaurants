Privacy notice

Under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”). Last updated: October 2026. The Italian version of this notice is the reference version.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the group of students running the Passaparola project, contact person: [Full name of the contact person], reachable at ciao@passaparola.it.

Passaparola is a student project born at Politecnico di Milano. Politecnico di Milano is neither the controller nor a processor of the data collected through this website.

2. What data we collect

We only collect the data you give us when filling in the application forms:

  • Restaurants: restaurant name, neighbourhood, contact details (email or phone), the restaurant's Instagram account.
  • Creators: Instagram or TikTok username, neighbourhood, favourite type of cuisine, email (optional).

We do not use profiling cookies or advertising trackers. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash: these providers may receive your IP address in order to serve the files.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • To contact you about your application to the pilot and, if you take part, to arrange visits between restaurants and creators. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into an agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not use your data for third-party marketing and we do not sell it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep the data for the duration of the pilot and in any case no longer than 12 months from collection, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

5. Who can see your data

Only members of the project team can access the data. It may be processed by technical providers that help us receive applications (for example our email or form service), acting as processors. For participating restaurants, we share with creators only the information needed to arrange the visit (the restaurant's name and address), and vice versa.

If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (for example adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses).

6. Your rights

You can at any time ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, obtain its portability and withdraw your consent (Articles 15–22 GDPR) by writing to ciao@passaparola.it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

7. Required fields

The fields marked as required in the forms are needed to assess your application and contact you: without them we cannot follow up.
