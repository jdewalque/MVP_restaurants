For restaurants

How it works for your restaurant

Three steps, nothing to worry about. You cook, we take care of the rest.

  1. 1

    We pick creators from your neighbourhood

    We select creators who live near you and speak to the people who live and work in your area.

  2. 2

    They come to eat and post

    Creators book a table, eat as regular customers and post a video about your place.

  3. 3

    You get the results

    We send you the links to the published videos and their view counts, so you can see what happened.

For creators

How it works if you create content

  • Invitations to restaurants near you

    Get offers from places in your own neighbourhood, not across town.

  • You choose the day and time

    Book the slot that suits you from those available. No chasing in DMs.

  • No negotiation

    The terms are clear from the start and the same for everyone. You just eat and tell the story.
